**Input → state → output trace (B02 → B08 → B09):**
1. **Input:** the J key (`p1_punch`, sent by the capture script through `Input.parse_input_event`);
   `PlayerController` starts a punch.
2. **State:** `check_hit()` finds the hitbox over Aotake's hurtbox and emits
   `attack_resolved(P1, "punch", "hit", point)` (`fighter.gd:170-172`); `main.gd` sets hitstop 4.
3. **Effect code:** `hit_spark_pool.gd` takes a free slot (`:43`), moves it to `point` (`:47`),
   `restart()`s it (`:48`); during hitstop and pause `:56` sets `speed_scale` 0.
4. **Scene:** `hit_spark.tscn` emits 20 stars at once, outward at 15-75 px/s, white to cyan, additive.
5. **Output:** a cyan burst around the old white code-drawn star at the contact point (B02, B09);
   it freezes while PAUSED is on screen and finishes after resume.
